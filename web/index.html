FOLLOW THE EVIDENCE

A discovery, not a claim.

These records matched place-related keyword queries in Europeana. A match may concern a person, event or a place with the same name. It does not prove that an image depicts the selected town.

The institution supplies the record. Its country is not necessarily the object's location. Open a record to examine its description, dates, recorded places and original source.

Small, deliberate collections

This prototype displays a dated, manually captured sample. Search and filters work within that sample; they do not query all of Europeana. Empty collections and missing dates remain visible.

Only records carrying a recognized Public Domain Mark, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA statement are included. Attribution and other licence conditions still apply. Metadata and media rights are different. Individual media files can carry different rights; verify the specific image at its original source before reuse.

Your collection stays with you

Saved items stay in this browser, including their source and capture date. Export citations before clearing browser storage. Images load from their published hosts; opening a source visits that institution. CoolCode counts aggregate page opens using the tool ID only. Searches, filtered links and saved collections are not sent to the counter. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected.
